Returns come from Yahoo Finance monthly prices adjusted for dividends and splits, so they are total returns that already include each fund's real fees. Deposits are made at the start of each month, and the current (incomplete) month is excluded. The fee-drag figure is only an estimate: it adds back each fund's CURRENT expense ratio for every month, but many funds charged more in the past. Annualized volatility is the standard deviation of monthly portfolio returns times the square root of 12 (past variability, not a forecast). Annualized growth rate and biggest drop are based on month-end values and adjusted for your deposits. When rebalancing is on, funds are reset to your target % each December with no trading costs or taxes. For educational purposes only. Historical returns do not guarantee future results.
